Privacy

How Little Lemon handles the information you share with us.

What we collect

Reservation, order, and newsletter forms collect your name, email, phone (optional), party details, and any notes you provide so we can confirm bookings and pickup orders.

How it’s used

Submissions are emailed to the restaurant via Formsubmit. We use them only to respond to your request. We do not sell personal information or use it for unrelated marketing without consent.

Storage

Your cart lives in your browser’s localStorage until you place or clear an order. Form emails are handled by Formsubmit’s service under their terms.
