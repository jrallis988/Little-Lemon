Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you visit this website or work together on a design project.

1. Information Collected

When you reach out for a project or fill out a contact form, you may provide basic contact details such as your name, email address, project details, and business information. This data is used solely to communicate with you, scope projects, and deliver design services.

2. Use of Information

Your information is strictly used for:

  • Communicating regarding active or prospective projects.
  • Fulfilling contractual agreements and delivering agreed-upon design assets.
  • Maintaining internal business and tax records.

Your information is never sold, rented, or shared with third-party marketers or data brokers.

3. Analytics and Cookies

This website may utilize lightweight, privacy-focused analytics to understand general traffic patterns and site performance without tracking your personal identity across the broader web.

4. Data Security

Appropriate administrative and technical measures are taken to protect your personal information and project files from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information held. To make a request, simply reach out via the contact form.
