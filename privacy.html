Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026 · Working Intelligence (“we”, “us”)

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit the Shift marketing site or join our early-access waitlist.

1. Information we collect

  • Waitlist details you submit: name, work email, company, role, and optional team/site size.
  • Usage data such as pages viewed, button clicks, and approximate timestamps — stored locally in your browser and optionally sent to our analytics provider if configured.
  • Technical data typically provided by your browser (user agent, language) when you load the site.

2. How we use information

  • To contact you about Shift pilots, demos, and product updates you requested.
  • To understand how the marketing site is used and improve it.
  • To secure the site and prevent abuse.

We do not sell personal information.

3. Workforce / employee data

This marketing site does not connect to your HRIS, ATS, or scheduling systems and does not process employee workforce data. When Shift launches as a product, customer agreements and a product-specific privacy notice will cover workforce data, integrations, retention, and subprocessors.

4. Cookies and tracking

We use first-party localStorage for waitlist confirmation and lightweight analytics. We honor Do Not Track where supported. If we enable a third-party analytics provider (for example Plausible), it will be configured for privacy-friendly measurement without advertising cookies.

5. Sharing

We may share information with service providers who help us operate the site or send waitlist communications, under confidentiality obligations. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Retention

Waitlist submissions are kept until you ask us to delete them, or until we close the waitlist and no longer need the records for pilot outreach (typically up to 24 months).

7. Your choices

  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your waitlist information.
  • Opt out of non-essential marketing emails using the unsubscribe link or by emailing us.

Contact: privacy@workingintelligence.com

8. Children

Shift is intended for business users. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as Shift moves from marketing site to product. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.
