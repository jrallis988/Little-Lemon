Privacy

This is a demonstration website. Forms do not transmit personal data to a server.

Any information you type into demo forms stays in your browser session for validation only and is discarded when the page resets. We do not operate a live mailing list from this prototype.

If this concept were production-ready, we would publish a full privacy policy covering data we collect, why we collect it, retention, and your rights.
