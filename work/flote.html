Case study

Seacoast Flōte

Full identity and booking experience for a Portsmouth & Hampton float therapy studio, with an accessibility system built to WCAG 2.1 AAA and kid-friendly content variants.

Role
Designer — Artistic Fountain (solo)
Discipline
UX · Brand
Type
Selected work

The problem

Float therapy is calm in person and noisy online — packages, locations, and first-time anxiety compete for attention. The studio needed a brand and a booking path that felt like the room: quiet, clear, and easy to trust before you ever lie down.

Approach

Work started with the guest journey, not the palette: who books, what they fear, and how a parent books for a child. Information architecture and wireframes locked the path, then identity (water, quiet, seacoast) was applied as a system — not decoration on a finished layout.

  • Guest and family journeys mapped before visual design
  • Booking flow reduced to the few decisions that matter
  • WCAG 2.1 AAA contrast, type, and content variants
  • Identity applied across web, wayfinding, and session materials

Outcome

A launch-ready identity and booking experience that treats accessibility as the system, not a pass at the end. Visuals for this study still need to be uploaded — screens and brand boards go in the frames below.
