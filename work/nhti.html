Case study

NHTI Identity Restoration

Editorial redesign for New Hampshire’s community college: restore the Lynx identity and connect the 1965 motto Scientia cum manu to the modern tagline in a maroon and gold system.

Role
Designer — Artistic Fountain (solo)
Discipline
Web · Identity
Type
Selected work

The problem

Institutional sites drift. Marks get redrawn, mottos drop out of the header, and a college starts to look like a template. NHTI already had history — the Lynx, Scientia cum manu — that was not carrying the homepage.

Approach

This restoration treats heritage as a design system: maroon and gold, the Lynx as a primary mark, and the 1965 motto as a living line next to the current tagline. Editorial layout (not a marketing carousel) does the work of sounding like a college.

  • Identity audit — what to restore vs. what to retire
  • Maroon & gold type, color, and photography rules
  • Homepage structure that leads with programs and place
  • Motto and tagline as one editorial system

Outcome

A coherent institutional look that can be applied to web first and print next. Upload the live pages, mark drawings, and type specimens into the frames below.
