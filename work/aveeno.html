Aveeno: The Oat Standard

Brand campaign concept positioning Aveeno’s oat heritage as a category standard — content system and campaign site, not a client commission.

Role
Designer — Artistic Fountain (solo)
Discipline
Social · Brand campaign
Type
Concept / pitch — not a client engagement

The problem

Oat is Aveeno’s proof, but category noise (clean, clinical, spa) can bury it. The concept treats oat not as a texture cue but as the standard the brand can own — heritage as a modern claim.

Approach

This is a speculative campaign, labeled as such. Work included a positioning line, a visual system for scaled social, and a campaign site scaffold so content could be templated instead of reinvented per post.

  • Positioning: heritage oat as the category standard
  • Campaign art direction and color/type rules
  • Social templates for feed, stories, and ads
  • Site structure for deploying the system at scale

Outcome

A campaign world a brand team could staff: line, look, and templates. Stills, grids, and motion frames go below when you have exports ready.
