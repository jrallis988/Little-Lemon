Case study Concept

Hannaford: Checkout-First

A speculative grocery-app strategy pitch: reframe shopping around a concierge checkout model, with a Fresh Pass paid tier, delivered as a production-ready HTML pitch site.

Role
Designer — Artistic Fountain (solo)
Discipline
Digital · Product strategy
Type
Concept / pitch — not a client engagement

The problem

Most grocery apps still start with aisles. Checkout, pickup, and membership sit at the end of the trip — after the customer has already done the work. This pitch asks what happens if concierge checkout is the product, not a footer link.

Approach

This is a strategy concept, not a commissioned Hannaford engagement. The work is a pitch narrative: research-backed reframing, a membership model (Fresh Pass), and a site you can click through instead of a slide deck.

  • Checkout-first information architecture
  • Concierge and pickup as the primary paths
  • Paid-tier story with clear member value
  • HTML pitch site so stakeholders can feel the flow

Outcome

A self-contained concept you can walk a hiring manager through: problem, model, and interface story. Screens and stills belong in the frames below when they are ready to publish.
