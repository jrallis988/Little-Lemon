Terms

Simple house rules for reservations and pickup orders.

Reservations

Tables are held for 15 minutes past the booked time. Large parties may require a deposit or earlier confirmation by phone.

Online orders

Online orders are for pickup only. Prices and availability may change. You’ll receive confirmation by email after Formsubmit delivers your request to the kitchen.

Allergies

Tell us about allergies in the notes field. We cook with shared equipment; we cannot guarantee a completely allergen-free kitchen.
