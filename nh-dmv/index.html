Transcript
  1. Choose the service you need — license, REAL ID, registration, tests, and more.
  2. Pick a branch and an available time. Same-day slots release daily when available.
  3. Run the readiness checklist so you arrive with the right documents.
  4. Hold your appointment in this portal and keep the confirmation on your dashboard.
