Four ways

Finish without the line when you can

Appointments cut wait times. Online, drop box, and mail handle many transactions without a counter visit.

01

Appointment

Required for most in-person services. Same-day slots release daily when available.

02

Online

Renewals, duplicates, driving records, tickets, and motorcycle training registration.

03

Drop box

Available at full-time offices for registrations, titles, and many paper transactions.

04

Mail

Eligible forms can be mailed — check the service page for addresses and checklists.

Updates

Important notices

Keep these on your radar before you visit or renew.

  • Inspection and emissions testing programs have ended. See inspections & emissions info.
  • Fee changes took effect January 1, 2026. Review common DMV fees, or call 603-227-4000 with questions.
  • The DMV is fully appointment-based for in-person services across all locations.

Federally required

Get your REAL ID

A REAL ID driver’s license, non-driver ID, or passport is required to board domestic flights and enter secure federal facilities.

  • Look for the star in the top-right corner of your credential
  • Bring original identity, Social Security, and residency documents
  • Use the online document checker before your visit

New to the Granite State?

New residents

60 days to get licensed & registered

Once you establish residency in New Hampshire, you have a maximum of 60 days to obtain an NH driver license and register your vehicles.
