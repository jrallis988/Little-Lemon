Make an appointment

The NH DMV is fully appointment-based for in-person services. Pick your transaction, choose a branch, and select a time — often in and out in 10–15 minutes.

Start booking on the official site Call 603-227-4000

How booking works

  • Select the service you need on the official Appointments & Services page.
  • Choose a convenient DMV location from 15 offices statewide.
  • Pick a day and time that works for you.
  • Check your inbox (and spam) for confirmation from noreply@dos.nh.gov.

Same-day options

Couldn’t plan ahead? Additional same-day appointments are released each day when available. Customer service can also help at 603-227-4000.

Common appointment services

  • REAL ID upgrade (outside of renewal)
  • Transfer license or ID from another state
  • New non-driver ID
  • Knowledge tests (driver, CDL, motorcycle)
  • Name or gender change
  • Many registration and title transactions

Already booked?

Verify, cancel, or reschedule from the official appointment tools. Bring required documents — especially for REAL ID and new-resident transfers.
