Celebrate America!

Authorized by HB 260. Cover plate for the front of your vehicle — keep your regular registration plate on the rear.

  • $25.00 per plate + $4.95 shipping
  • Display window: Jan 1, 2026 – Jul 4, 2027 (or keep as a souvenir)
  • Up to 4 plates per online order · bulk 25+ at Concord (23 Hazen Drive)
  • Credit card only · purchases are non-refundable
  • Proceeds support LCHIP historic & natural resource preservation

What you need to know

Cover plate only

Replaces the front plate. Your rear registration plate must remain your official issued plate.

Ships to you

Order online with a credit card — no counter visit. Allow up to 4 weeks for production & shipping.

Display period

Legal front-plate display runs Jan 1, 2026 through Jul 4, 2027. Orders online until Jul 4, 2027.

Where the money goes

Proceeds benefit New Hampshire’s Land and Community Heritage Investment Program (LCHIP).

Who can buy

Anyone residing in the United States. Purchases are non-refundable.

Bulk orders

Orders of 25 or more commemorative plates: visit Concord DMV at 23 Hazen Drive.
