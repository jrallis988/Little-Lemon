NEW TO NEW HAMPSHIRE?

Welcome. Answer a few questions and we’ll build a simple plan for your license, vehicle, title, and residency documents — so you know what to do in your first 60 days.

Do you already have a valid out-of-state driver license?

New residents generally have 60 days after establishing NH residency to transfer.

Are you bringing a vehicle to New Hampshire?

Registration timing depends on whether the vehicle is already titled and insured in your name.

Do you need a New Hampshire title?

Titles are required for most vehicles. Bring ownership and lien documents if applicable.

Do you already have residency documents?

REAL ID and many credentials need two proofs of NH residency (utility bill, lease, bank statement, etc.).
