Stay safe

Scam alert

The New Hampshire DMV never texts residents about payments. Here’s how to spot and avoid common DMV-related scams.

What the NH DMV will never do

  • Send text messages asking you to pay a fine, fee, or “unlock” your license
  • Ask you to click a link in a text or email to pay immediately
  • Demand gift cards, wire transfers, or cryptocurrency
  • Threaten arrest over the phone for unpaid “DMV tickets”

What to do if you get a suspicious message

  1. Do not click links or call numbers in the message.
  2. Verify through this portal or by calling 603-227-4000.
  3. Never share Social Security numbers, bank details, or one-time codes with unexpected callers.
  4. Report suspected scams to local law enforcement when appropriate.
