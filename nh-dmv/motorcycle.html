Testing & training

Motorcycle rider training

Seasonal courses, then knowledge and skills testing — same path the live NH DMV highlights for new riders.

How the path works

  1. Review the season calendar and pick a training site.
  2. Register for the course (online when the season is open).
  3. Complete classroom / range training.
  4. Finish knowledge and skills testing, then add the endorsement.

Credential fees (as of Jan 1, 2026)

  • Motorcycle only or motor driven cycle only — $55.00
  • First-time motorcycle or 3-wheel endorsement — $30.00
  • Renewal of motorcycle or 3-wheel endorsement — $5.00

Training course tuition is set by the training provider — not the DMV counter fee schedule.
