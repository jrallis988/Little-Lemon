Learning at Home

Short activities that pair with favorite shows — counting, kindness, and curiosity.

  • After Blue’s Clues: draw three clues from your day
  • After Dora: practice one new map word
  • After Bubble Guppies: splash-safe science in the sink

Screen Time Tips

Simple guardrails that keep Nick Jr. moments joyful for kids and adults.

  • Co-view when you can — ask “what happens next?”
  • Use Play & Learn games as a bridge off video
  • End on a Create activity, not a cliffhanger

Ages & Stages

Find shows and games that match where your little learner is right now.

  • Ages 2–3: songs, colors, gentle stories
  • Ages 3–5: numbers, letters, problem solving
  • Ages 5–7: longer adventures and crafts
