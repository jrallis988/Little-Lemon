Convening a conversation. Building what comes next.

Whole Youth exists to understand the many factors around youth mental health and wellbeing — then strengthen the communities that help young people flourish.

Our approach

Listen widely. Act locally.

We bring together mental health experts, parents, youth, nonprofit partners, educators, and policymakers. The goal is not a single silver bullet — it is a fuller picture of isolation, device use, family stress, violence exposure, play, and school support, so solutions match real life.

That conversation feeds practical work: places to belong, coaches in classrooms, and resources families can trust.

“It’s like solving a symptom, but not necessarily the problem.”
— Parent voice from our listening research

Research

What parents are telling us

Across states, families ask for nuance: more digital literacy, more adult guidance, and caution about one-size bans.

Nearly nine in ten want safe tech education

Parents strongly support teaching young people how to use emerging tools — including AI — safely and responsibly.

Heavy bans raise unintended risks

Many parents worry that restricting phones without broader support can affect safety, communication, and preparedness.

It takes a village

Families want schools, community programs, and mentors working together — not isolated quick fixes.

Resources for caregivers

Parents say they need clearer education and tools to guide children’s technology use at home.

Coalition partners

Many voices, one urgency

We coordinate perspectives so young people are not asked to navigate wellbeing alone.
