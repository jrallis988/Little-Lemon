Video as the New Design Canvas

June 2026

Motion graphics and animated design aren’t extras anymore—they’re essential.

Motion graphics and animated design aren’t extras anymore—they’re essential. From micro-interactions to full animated explainers, video elevates digital experiences and drives engagement.

Design that moves

Static layouts still matter, but attention often lands on what moves. Motion can explain a product faster than a paragraph, guide a user through a flow, or give a brand a living personality.

Where it belongs

  • Product UI: subtle states and feedback that make interactions feel clear.
  • Brand films & explainers: After Effects-led storytelling for campaigns and launches.
  • Social: short loops and templates built for the platforms your audience actually uses.

Keep craft in the loop

AI can help explore variants quickly. Finishing still happens in tools like After Effects, Premiere, and your brand system—so motion stays on-brand, not generic.
