Our Animation Process

A professional production pipeline built for clarity, collaboration, and cinematic results.

  1. 01

    Animation Production Brief

    We establish a creative design brief to define the narrative, tone, and visual style, translating your concept into early storyboard art and character sketches.

  2. 02

    Storyboard & Timing

    A sequential storyboard is drawn up to map out camera angles, visual pacing, and exact motion timing.

  3. 03

    Animation & Motion Graphics

    Animatic production and full 2D character animation or motion graphics are brought to life frame by frame.

  4. 04

    Sound Sync & Final Render

    Final adjustments are made to motion and timing before exporting master video files in your required professional output formats.

Storyboarding

Every successful animation begins with careful planning. Storyboards define composition, camera angles, pacing, and visual storytelling before a single frame is animated — so the team (and the client) share one picture of the film.

We board for clarity: shot intent, performance notes, transitions, and continuity. Strong boards save time downstream and protect the story when production gets complex.

Character Design

Characters carry the performance. We design for personality and production — silhouettes that read, faces that act, and assets that animate cleanly.

  • Turnarounds — front, three-quarter, side, and back views for consistency
  • Expression sheets — emotional range locked to the character’s design language
  • Pose exploration — attitude, rhythm, and acting ideas before rigging
  • Rig-ready artwork — clean separations and construction for Harmony pipelines

2D Animation

Performance-driven 2D work — from single acting beats to full scene production. We focus on timing, weight, and emotion so characters feel alive on screen.

  • Character acting — intention, timing, and emotional beats
  • Walk cycles — locomotion with personality
  • Lip sync — dialogue that matches performance
  • Acting performance — poses and transitions that sell the moment
  • Scene animation — multi-element shots with clear staging
  • Background animation — environments that support, not distract
