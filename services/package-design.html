Service

Package Design

Shelf-ready packaging designed on real manufacturer die-lines — structural precision with brand storytelling that pops from a distance.

How it works

01

Packaging & Dieline Brief

We begin with a structural design brief reviewing your product physical dimensions, packaging material constraints, and target retail shelf environment.

02

Die-Line Mapping

Structural concepts and layout grids are built directly onto precise manufacturer die-lines to ensure proper folding, sizing, and bleed.

03

Shelf-Impact Styling

Typography, regulatory compliance info, and striking graphics are applied to maximize visual pop and brand storytelling from a distance.

04

Print-Ready Handoff

Final vector files, color separations, and plate specifications are prepared and delivered straight to your manufacturer.

Toolkit

We engineer exact structural dielines and vector packaging flats in Illustrator, then bring them into Adobe Dimension to generate photorealistic 3D mockups. This allows you to see shelf-ready boxes, bottles, and labels in real-world lighting environments before signing off on physical print production.
