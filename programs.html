Programs that meet young people where they are

From neighborhood gyms and camps to school-day coaching and caregiver workshops — one continuum of support.

Offerings

Designed for belonging, learning, and balance

Community hubs

Fitness, aquatics, arts, teen centers, and family nights — welcoming spaces where strangers become friends and every age finds a place to thrive.

Camps & outdoors

Day and overnight experiences that build confidence, teamwork, and lifelong friendships away from the scroll.

Student success coaches

Near-peer AmeriCorps-style members embed in public schools to support attendance, behavior, coursework, and social-emotional skills — helping students engage more deeply with learning.

After-school learning labs

Homework help, tutoring, and project clubs that keep kids connected to caring adults after the bell.

Wellbeing workshops

Conversation circles and caregiver nights on digital literacy, sleep, stress, and asking for help — grounded in research, not panic.

Youth leadership

Service projects and peer mentoring that turn young people into the change they want to see in their own communities.
