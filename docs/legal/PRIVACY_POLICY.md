# Trump RX — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: June 11, 2026**

TrumpRx is committed to transparency about how we handle your information.
This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your
choices regarding that data.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Location Data

- **Automatic (IP-based):** Hosting provider (Cloudflare) may detect approximate
  location to show local pharmacies.
- **Browser Geolocation:** Only with explicit permission (“Use my current
  location”).
- **User-Entered:** ZIP code entry.
- Location preference may be stored in browser local storage on your device.

### Email Address

- Stored if you sign up for medication / price alerts. Unsubscribe anytime.

### Account data (when you create an account)

- Email, optional name/phone, saved medications, preferred pharmacies, coupons,
  membership status — stored to provide the Service.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect (as a discount card)

We do not require Social Security numbers or government IDs. Payment for
prescriptions is made directly with pharmacies. Plus membership payments are
processed by Stripe (see Stripe’s privacy policy).

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- Location: show nearby pharmacies
- Email / phone: alert delivery if you opt in
- Account: sync saved meds, coupons, membership

## 4. Cookies & Local Storage

Local storage may remember ZIP / location preference. Clear via browser settings.

## 5. Third-Party Services

- Cloudflare — hosting / edge
- AWS Location Service (or equivalent geocoding) — ZIP / address resolution
- Stripe — Plus billing (when enabled)
- Resend — transactional email (when enabled)
- Twilio — SMS alerts (when enabled)

Review each provider’s privacy policy.

## 6. Data Retention

- Local storage: until cleared
- Alert signups / account data: until unsubscribe, deletion request, or account
  closure (subject to legal retention needs)

## 7. Security

HTTPS in transit, rate limiting, minimal collection practices.

## 8. Your Rights & Choices

Clear local storage; deny geolocation; use pricing without email; unsubscribe;
request account deletion via support. **CCPA / GDPR placeholders:** we will
honor applicable access, correction, and deletion requests as we expand
jurisdictions.

## 9. Changes

We will update the “Last Updated” date when this policy changes materially.
