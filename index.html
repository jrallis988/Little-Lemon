Who we are

A coalition for the whole young person

Youth wellbeing is shaped by more than any single issue. We bring parents, students, mental health experts, educators, and community leaders into one urgent conversation — then turn insight into local support.

  • Social isolation and the need for real-world belonging
  • Device use, digital literacy, and healthy tech habits
  • Family and economic stress that follows kids into school
  • Learning environments where every student can stay on track
“There are no shortcuts. When we invest in belonging, coaching, and care together, young people show us what becomes possible.”
— Whole Youth Coalition

How we help

Belong. Become. Balance.

Three connected pillars — inspired by community centers, service in schools, and honest dialogue about mental health.

Belong

Places to grow together

After-school clubs, camps, sports, and family programs where unlikely friendships form and every generation finds a place — the everyday fabric of stronger communities.

Become

Support that meets students where they learn

Near-peer coaches and tutors partner with public schools so students build attendance, confidence, and the durable skills that help them become who they’ll be tomorrow.

Balance

Mental wellbeing without oversimplification

We listen to parents and youth about technology, isolation, and stress — then advance nuanced solutions that protect wellbeing without pretending one ban fixes everything.
