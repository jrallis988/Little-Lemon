    Discovery & Strategy

    My Full Story

    Who I Am

    I'm James, a designer based in New Hampshire, running Artistic Fountain as a solo studio.

    My work spans brand systems, product strategy, and full-stack web — from institutional identity redesigns for schools like NHTI and Great Bay Community College, to speculative rebrands for national brands like Gap, Old Navy, and Aveeno, to production HTML for launch-ready sites like Seacoast Flōte.

    My background spans creative direction, full-stack development, marketing analytics, and business operations. That means I don't design in isolation — I understand how design intersects with technology, strategy, and results.

    My Design Philosophy

    I believe in intentional systems over arbitrary aesthetics. Every color choice, typeface, interaction, and layout decision should serve a purpose. Good design isn't about being pretty—it's about being smart, scalable, and user-centered.

    I focus on:

    • Strategic Clarity: Understanding the problem before proposing solutions.
    • User-Centric Thinking: Designing for real people with real needs, not for award submissions.
    • Systems & Scalability: Building design systems that work across platforms, contexts, and time.
    • Technical Integration: Ensuring design translates flawlessly into code and implementation.
    • Data-Informed Decisions: Using analytics and user feedback to refine and optimize continuously.
