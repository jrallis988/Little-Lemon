Our story

A neighborhood kitchen with a mango heart

Red Mango began as a family table—recipes carried from Chengdu and Guangzhou, then tuned for the people who eat with us every week. We cook with a hot wok, honest seasoning, and one sweet rule: leave room for something bright at the end.

以热锅、真心、一颗芒果的甜意待客。
