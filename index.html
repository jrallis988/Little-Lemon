How a story grows

One clear path from spark to screen — built for collaborators and curious kids alike.

  1. 01

    Spark

    Find the feeling first — wonder, giggles, bravery — then the plot.

  2. 02

    Sketch

    Flat shapes, bold color keys, and boards that read from across the room.

  3. 03

    Animate

    Timing with bounce, soft arcs, and expressions kids can mirror.

  4. 04

    Share

    Polish, sound, and a delivery ready for classrooms and living rooms.
