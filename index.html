The Designer

I'm James, a New Hampshire designer running Artistic Fountain as a one-person studio.

I work across brand systems, product strategy, and full-stack web — from institutional identity work like NHTI and Great Bay, to speculative rebrands for consumer brands, to production HTML for launch-ready sites.

My work is defined by strategic depth over surface polish. Every project starts with the problem, not the palette. If you're building something and want a designer who ships, not just decks, let's talk.

How I Work

Intentional systems over arbitrary aesthetics — a clear process from discovery through launch.

01

Discovery & Strategy

I begin by understanding your brand, audience, and market position. Through deep market research, competitive analysis, and customer discovery, I build the strategic foundation for every design decision. No assumptions—just informed strategy grounded in real data.

02

Concept & Creation

With strategy in place, I create visual identities, design systems, and digital experiences. This includes everything from typography and color theory to wireframes, 3D modeling, AI-enhanced assets, and interactive prototypes. Every element is intentional and aligned with your brand goals.

03

Technical Execution

I deliver production-ready assets and full-stack web development solutions. From React and responsive design to backend infrastructure, I ensure your concepts translate flawlessly into code. Your designs don't just look beautiful—they perform.

04

Optimization & Launch

Post-launch, I implement analytics, run A/B testing, and track KPIs to optimize performance. I refine based on real user data, ensuring your design continues to deliver results. Continuous improvement is built into every project.
