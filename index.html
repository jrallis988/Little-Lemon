Our story

Family-owned Asian cuisine in Stratham

Red Mango Asian Cuisine is a husband-and-wife kitchen on Portsmouth Avenue, serving Chinese and Japanese dishes with fresh ingredients and a modern, cozy dining room. Locals come for lunch plates, sushi, takeout, and dinner—hot, quick, and welcoming.

FAQ

Before you visit

Where do I park?
We’re in Kings Highway Plaza at 28 Portsmouth Ave. Free plaza parking is right outside Suite 8.
Do you deliver?
Yes—order online for delivery or pickup. Delivery fees and distance limits depend on your address.
How spicy is the food?
Tell us your preference when you order. Many dishes can be mild, medium, or hot.
Vegetarian options?
Yes—vegetable dishes, tofu specialties, veggie lo mein, and vegetarian sushi rolls are on the menu.
Large orders or parties?
Call ahead for party trays, sushi boats, and catering-sized orders so we can prepare on time.
