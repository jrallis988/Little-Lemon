The studio

Playhouse Animation makes flat, friendly 2D stories for young audiences — bold color, clear emotion, and characters kids want to hug.

  • For little screens

    Preschool and early elementary films built to read on tablets, TVs, and classroom displays.

  • Flat & joyful

    Paper-cutout energy, sticker-bright palettes, and acting that feels like Saturday morning.

  • Story first

    Wonder, giggles, and bravery lead the way — then boards, color, and final frames follow.

How a story grows

One clear path from spark to screen — built for collaborators and curious kids alike.
