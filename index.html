Our story

Family-owned Asian cuisine in Stratham

Red Mango Asian Cuisine is a husband-and-wife kitchen on Portsmouth Avenue, serving Chinese and Japanese dishes with fresh ingredients and a modern, cozy dining room. Locals come for lunch plates, sushi, takeout, and dinner—hot, quick, and welcoming.
