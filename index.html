Questions?

Do managers need to learn prompting?

No. Describe your workforce goals — Shift agents run the workflows and only escalate when they need approval or judgment.

Does this replace our HRIS or scheduling tools?

No. Shift connects to the systems you already use and works inside those workflows instead of forcing a rip-and-replace.

Who has final say on actions?

You do. Agents propose actions with Approve, Snooze, or Decline controls — especially for employee-facing or compliance-sensitive work.

What kinds of teams is Shift built for?

Multi-location, shift-heavy, and high-turnover workforces — retail, hospitality, logistics, healthcare ops, and field teams.

How fast can we roll out?

Start with one site or department, connect calendars and your ATS/HRIS, and expand once managers feel the coverage lift.
