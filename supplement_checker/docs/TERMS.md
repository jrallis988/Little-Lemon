- **NOT** a medical device  
- **NOT** a diagnostic tool  
- **NOT** a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment  

Always consult a qualified physician or specialist regarding supplements, medications, or conditions.

## 2. Mandatory Gaps & Knowledge Limits notice

Before uploading medical history or accessing scan/analysis features, you must accept the un-skippable Gaps & Knowledge Limits notice. Key points include:

- Literature and databases are incomplete and dynamic  
- Unindexed ingredients receive **no** safety or mechanistic evaluation  
- Absence of a flag does **not** indicate safety  
- Outputs are exploratory research projections only
