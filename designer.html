About

The Designer

Strategic design from a one-person New Hampshire studio — identity systems, digital experiences, and production-ready delivery.

I'm James, a New Hampshire designer running Artistic Fountain as a one-person studio.

I work across brand systems, product strategy, and full-stack web — from institutional identity work like NHTI and Great Bay, to speculative rebrands for consumer brands, to production HTML for launch-ready sites.

My work is defined by strategic depth over surface polish. Every project starts with the problem, not the palette. If you're building something and want a designer who ships, not just decks, let's talk.
