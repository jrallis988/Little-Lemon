photosynthesis
/ˌfoʊ.toʊˈsɪn.θə.sɪs/

Photosynthesis is how plants make their own food using sunlight, water, and air.

Example: Leaves use photosynthesis to turn sunlight into energy for the plant.
✨ AI Instant Answer

A kid-friendly answer

Photosynthesis is the special way green plants make food. They catch sunlight with their leaves, mix it with water from the ground and a gas from the air called carbon dioxide, then make sugar for energy and release oxygen that people and animals breathe.
