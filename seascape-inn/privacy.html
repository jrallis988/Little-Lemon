Privacy Policy

How we handle information you share when you contact or book with Seascape Inn.

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Seascape Inn at Plaice Cove (“we,” “us”) operates this website to share information about our motel and to receive guest inquiries.

Information we collect

When you use our contact or booking inquiry forms, or email/call us directly, you may provide:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Stay dates, guest count, and room preferences
  • Any details you include in your message

Our website may also collect standard technical data through your browser (such as IP address and pages visited) via hosting or analytics tools if those are enabled in the future.

How we use information

We use inquiry details to respond to your questions, quote rates, confirm reservations, and communicate about your stay. We do not sell your personal information.

Email forms

Contact and booking forms on this site open your email application with a pre-filled message. Message content is sent through your email provider to seascapeinnhampton@gmail.com.

Third-party services

This site may load fonts from Google Fonts, maps from Google Maps, and placeholder images from image hosts. Those providers may process technical data according to their own policies. Links to Facebook or TripAdvisor are optional and governed by those services.

Data retention

We keep reservation and inquiry correspondence as needed for guest service and business records, then delete or archive it according to ordinary motel record-keeping practices.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can contact us to update or clarify information you have shared, or to ask how an inquiry was handled.
