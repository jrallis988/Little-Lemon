Good to know

Stay details

Check-in & check-out

Rooms are ready at 3:00 PM. Please check out by 10:00 AM so we can prepare for the next guests.

Pet policy

Dogs up to 40 lbs are welcome in designated rooms for a $25/week pet fee. Please keep pets leashed in common areas and use the pet park on site.

Parking

Free on-site parking is included — one space per room. Additional vehicles may be limited during peak season; call ahead if you need more than one space.

Seasonal rates

Published starting rates are off-season. Summer and holiday weekends are higher. Call for exact quotes for your dates.
